## Reasoning
The combination is characteristic: **a safe bottom strip, several horizontal moving-object lanes, a fixed top destination, and a strong preference for upward progress with lateral adjustments**.

That fits road crossing much better than Snake or Pac-Man, which normally involve distributed collectible targets; maze navigation, which would require structured interior obstacles; or Pong/Breakout, whose controlled paddle normally occupies a restricted line. The repeated lane patterns particularly suggest spaced traffic.

Weights cannot uniquely establish a game’s name or exact reward rules, but these spatial signatures strongly identify the game family.
