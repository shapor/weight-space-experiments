## Observations

These are **weight-based hypotheses, not measured attention patterns**. I used only matrix algebra; no forward passes or searches.

For compactness, define \(P=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top/384\), \(D_l=\operatorname{diag}(\text{ln1_w.l})\), and, for each head,
\[
M=P D_lW_q^\top W_kD_lP/8,\qquad A=W_oW_vD_lP.
\]
These are static weight products, not evaluations of LayerNorm or attention.

- **L0H3 looks like a positional character-transport head.** Its leading 20 query/key right-singular subspaces overlap by **0.717**, versus only **0.027** for query/value subspaces. Thus its addressing and transported information are substantially separated. The raw \(W_q^\top W_k/8\) has prominent rotation-like eigenpairs, including **\(0.724\pm0.691i\)** and **\(0.446\pm0.842i\)**. This suggests a positional offset, most plausibly the previous character.
- **L0H0 appears to use a longer local offset.** Within L0H3’s leading shared query/key subspace, its matrix aligns better with powers 3–5 of L0H3’s polar factor than with power 1: cosine **0.201, 0.239, 0.145**, versus **−0.264**. These are not literal measured offsets.
- **L0H5 looks like broad pooling, particularly of position-related information.** Its \(\|M\|_F\) is just **0.0339**, while \(\|A\|_F=18.812\). Its two leading raw OV read directions have **0.839** and **0.551** squared overlap with the position-like subspace identified above.
- **Most middle-layer heads reuse this early positional representation.** Define the static key-preference vector
  \[
  b=P D_lW_k^\top W_q\,\text{ln1_b.l}/8.
  \]
  In layer 1, **79–85%** of its squared norm lies in L0H5’s leading two write directions; in layer 2, **61–85%** does. Their directional matrix components also align with L0H3’s through the L0H5 pathway. This supports shared positional/recency machinery rather than six unrelated mechanisms.
- **L3H3 is the clearest induction candidate.** The composition statistic
  \[
  c=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(M A_{\mathrm{L0H3}})}
          {\|M A_{\mathrm{L0H3}}\|_F}
  \]
  is **0.924** there, versus at most **0.218** for any other head in layers 1–3. It also largely avoids the shared positional key-preference directions.
- **Corpus evidence is weaker.** `W_proj.3` concentrates 90% of its squared singular-value mass in **22 directions**. L0H3’s value spectrum also has a relatively compact strong component. This weakly favors romaji’s smaller effective alphabet, but neither statistic identifies characters or proves an alphabet size.

## Reasoning

**As-is text fits substantially better than shuffled text.** A position-offset transport head, another apparent local-offset head, extensive downstream positional machinery, and a distinct induction candidate collectively suggest exploitation of ordered sequences—not merely line-level character counts. Shuffling preserves useful line structure, so it is not impossible, but it is a poorer explanation.

My best corpus guess is **romaji**: the predominantly local organization and comparatively compact strong value/output spectra fit short-range romanized phonotactics and a smaller effective alphabet. English remains a serious alternative. Python is less favored, but I cannot identify indentation, particular letters, or keywords from these measurements.

The corpus probabilities are therefore deliberately less decisive than the order probabilities. Exact attention distances remain unverified without the missing embeddings and prohibited activation measurements.
