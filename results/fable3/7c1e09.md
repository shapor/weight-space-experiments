Observations:
- Layer 0 is positional. ln1_w.0 is tiny on most dims (mean 0.27, 53 dims < 0.1) and ~1 on ~40 dims (211, 273, 223, 180, 50, 162, ...). With ln1_w folded in, the Q and K row spaces of L0H1 and L0H5 coincide (overlap 0.56 / 0.74 vs 0.04 random) and lie in that emphasised subspace ("Pos": 86-95% of their Q/K energy), while their V reads a disjoint subspace ("T": V/K overlap 0.05). Their QK matrices (|QK/8|_F = 0.88, 0.97, top singular values 0.6 / 0.41) have complex leading eigenvalues (0.22 +/- 0.35j, 0.21 +/- 0.27j): a rotation-like bilinear form on positional features = relative-offset attention. Their OV circuits are flat-spectrum, 90%-energy rank 20-21: they copy a full token identity from a fixed relative position. MLP0 (W_fc.0 * ln2_w.0) reads T at 3x baseline and Pos at 0.5x baseline, confirming T = token identity, Pos = position.
- Two layer-0 heads have essentially zero QK: L0H4 (|QK/8|_F = 0.01, |W_q| = 0.6) and L0H0 (0.11). They attend uniformly over the prefix. Their OV is rank 2 (L0H4 singular values 13.9, 9.7, 2.5; L0H0 3.4, 2.5, 0.5), V reads Pos (0.84, 0.70) and the output lands in dims 273, 21, 123, 307, 51, 125, 35, which are exactly the dims where ln1_b.1 / ln2_b.0-3 have their largest entries. That 2-dim prefix-average-of-position signal is read almost only by the K side of all six L1 heads (36-90x the random read fraction; Q 2-3x, V 0.1-0.4x) and by K of L2H0/H2/H3/H4/H5 (8-24x): a recency bias for keys.
- L1 heads are six near-copies of one design: K reads the recency scalar, Q reads the current token (T at 2.5x, L0H5's output at 3x, MLP0 token-neuron outputs at 3-5x), V reads the L0H5/L0H1 previous-token copies (V-composition 2-6x baseline) and the current token. Their QK restricted to the token subspace is strongly negative-definite (tr/|G| = -2.2 to -2.4; 3-4 of 20 eigenvalues positive): they avoid keys carrying the same character as the query. All six (and L2H2, L2H3, L2H4, L2H5, L3H2, L2H0) share one bias-driven key-token direction (pairwise cosines 0.8-0.94): a single boundary character (almost certainly the space) organises local attention.
- Induction circuit: F = T * QK_B * OV_L0H5 * T^T (query = current token, key = previous token copied by L0H5) is strongly positive-definite for L2H1 (tr/|F| = +2.90, 17/20 positive eigenvalues), L3H1 (+2.70), L3H5 (+2.87) and weaker for L3H0 (+1.17); the random expectation is |tr/|F|| ~ 0.22. With L0H1 as the source the test is at noise level, so L0H5 is the previous-token head that feeds induction. L2H2 is anti-induction (-1.89). L3H5 also matches same-token (duplicate test +1.63) and has an all-positive QK spectrum (trace +8.05).
- Alphabet size: a token-like subspace (read by L0 V and MLP0, not by L0 Q/K) shows 27 strong singular values of the L0H1/L0H5 V matrices (0.63 ... 0.235) followed by a cliff (0.073, 0.059, 0.052): 27 trained token directions = a full 28-symbol alphabet is in use.
- Layers 2-3 QK and OV are high-rank (effective rank 51-61 of 64) and read the raw token/position subspaces only at baseline: generic contextual heads on MLP-re-encoded features.

Reasoning:
The network has the full as-is text toolkit: a sharp previous-token head (L0H5) whose copied token identity is matched against the current token by three clear induction heads (L2H1, L3H1, L3H5, tr/|F| ~ +2.8 against a noise level of 0.2), a second fixed-offset copy head (L0H1), MLP0/MLP1 reading those previous-character copies (1.2-2x baseline), and six L1 heads whose queries depend on the current character and whose values gather (char, previous char) pairs. None of this has any value on lines whose characters are shuffled (there the previous character and any repeated substring carry no information; only the bag of characters and the distance to the last newline do), so the training text was used as is. For the corpus: the alphabet in use has 27 token directions (a 28-symbol vocabulary), which argues against Hepburn romaji (l, q, v, x never occur, c only in "ch", so ~24 symbols). Between Python and English: there is exactly one shared boundary-character key direction organising ten heads (the word separator), no second one that would serve newline/indentation, which Python's short indented lines would need; induction appears only in layers 2-3 and in 3-4 of 24 heads, while a code model (identifier copying is most of the predictable signal) would make induction earlier and more dominant; and most heads are local word-internal n-gram heads with a recency bias, which fits natural-language prose. So English Wikipedia, as is.
