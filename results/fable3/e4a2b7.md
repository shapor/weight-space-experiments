Observations:
All analysis is on the block weights only (LN weights folded into W_q/W_k/W_v/W_fc as W*diag(ln_w)); no forward passes. 12 code runs.

1. Layer 0 is small and asymmetric. ln1_w.0 has mean 0.30 (later layers 0.5-0.7). Head norms: L0H3 is by far the strongest (|W_q|=14.1, |W_k|=14.7 vs 2.6-7 for the other L0 heads); its QK circuit W_q^T W_k has ~27 nearly equal singular values (8.9, 8.7, 8.6, 8.5 ... raw; 1.95 ... 1.0 after LN folding) and its OV circuit W_o W_v has rank ~30 (sv 1.13, 0.96, 0.95, ...). L0H5 has an almost empty QK (top sv 1.44 raw, 0.26 after LN) but a huge OV (sv 24.8, 12.9, 5.9; effective rank 5.7). L0H1/H2/H4 have rank-1/2 QK (top sv 6.7, 4.4, 4.1 raw, with a single negative real eigenvalue) and rank-2 OV (sv 5.7/3.4, 3.9/2.4, 2.5/1.3).
2. Two input subspaces can be separated without the embeddings. Define P = top-30 right-singular subspace of [W_q.0;W_k.0] of head 3, T = top-28 right-singular subspace of W_v.0 of head 3. MLP0 (W_fc.0) reads T at 1.9x the random level and P at 0.64x (below random), and reads the output subspace of L0H3 (O3 = range of W_o W_v of head 3) at 1.8x; MLP0 ignores P entirely (no neuron has >30% of its input energy in P). The natural reading is P = positional subspace, T = character-identity subspace: the MLP of layer 0 reads character identity (current and previous) and not position.
3. L0H3's QK bilinear form puts 92% of its energy in the P x P block (random level 0.6%), and restricted to P it is a rotation: eigenvalue pairs of modulus 1.55, 1.15, 1.10, 1.08, 1.06 at angles +/-45, 39, 46, 51, 61 degrees, i.e. the same phase advance on several 2-D planes. That is a fixed-offset positional pattern on a helical position code, and its V copies character identity (V reads T). L0H0: QK energy 58% in P x P with real positive top eigenvalues (0.63, 0.39: affinity to similar positions = nearby), V reads T at 2.3x random.
4. L0H1, H2, H4, H5 are one family: their OV outputs share one 2-3 dimensional subspace U (98%, 97%, 93%, 100% of their OV output energy lies in the top-8 singular directions of the concatenation; the concatenation's sv are 16.5, 9.8, 3.2, then <1.2), their OV input directions overlap 0.6-0.8 pairwise, these input directions lie 83% inside P (8% in T), their own queries read the same directions (6-8x random) and their values read P (2.5-3.2x random). So these four heads aggregate (H5 essentially uniformly) a 2-D projection of the position embedding over the context and amplify it (OV gain up to 24.8): they manufacture a cleaned "where am I in the window" code. This code U is read almost exclusively by keys of later heads: all six L1 heads read U at 4.1-4.9x random in W_k (Q 1.5x, V 0.8x), L2 heads 2.6-3.8x, L3H0/H1/H2/H5 2.0-2.3x, MLP1-3 1.0x. K-composition scores L0->L1 (Elhage-style, baseline 0.05): 0.36-0.51 from H1/H2/H4/H5, 0.03-0.07 from H0/H3. All other inter-layer composition scores (L1->L2, L1->L3, L2->L3, Q/K/V) are at the random baseline 0.05.
5. MLP0 is a bigram detector bank. 317 of 1536 neurons put >20% of their input energy in both T (current character) and O3 (previous character, via H3). Mapping the O3 part back to the token basis through W_o W_v of H3 and comparing with the T part, the cosine between the two readouts is centred at 0 (mean -0.01, sd 0.33), so these neurons read (current, previous) as genuine pairs, not as an additive count of the same character. Both readouts have effective rank ~22-24.
6. Layer 1: the six heads are near clones (QK norms 15-17, QK top sv 20-25 raw, pairwise QK cosine 0.3-0.4, OV ranks 25-30). Their top two QK singular pairs are position-code vs position-code (K side 83-87% in U, Q side in U and in the MLP0 output subspace); restricted to U x U the form is rank 2 with a complex pair at ~72 degrees in every L1 head (and ~75 degrees in every L2 head). The content part is self-avoiding: in the T basis the T x T block has a negative diagonal (mean -0.10 to -0.21 vs off-diagonal sd 0.06-0.09), the previous-character block likewise (trace/Frobenius -0.5 to -2.5), and the self-score of every strong MLP0 feature direction d^T QK d is negative (mean -0.09 to -0.11 vs off-diagonal sd 0.09). The "induction" block (query current char vs key previous char, T x OV3T) is only weakly positive (diag 0.02-0.03, ~2.4 sigma in aggregate). Their V reads MLP0 output (55-62%), O3 (22-32%) and T (13-20%).
7. All 18 OV circuits in layers 1-3 are "negative identity" on their input subspace: nearly all eigenvalues real and negative (e.g. L1H0: -1.1, -1.1, -1.0, -1.0, ...; L3H3: -3.0, -3.0, -2.9, -2.5 ...; traces -15 to -65). The heads write back the negated features of what they attend to; the sign relative to the unembedding is not recoverable without W_out.
8. Layer 2 heads: QK top pairs again position-code based (K side 29-57% in U) with negative self terms (same previous character block trace/F -0.6 to -1.9), V reads the MLP1 output subspace (32-54%).
9. Layer 3: L3H3 has a positive-definite QK over ~63 dimensions (trace +46.6, eigenvalues 1.5, 1.5, 1.4 ... all real positive, top singular pairs with cos(u,v)=+0.3 to +0.5, zero use of U): it attends to positions whose representation matches the current one. In the T basis its query-current-char vs key-previous-char block is identity-like: diagonal mean 0.158 vs off-diagonal mean 0.001, sd 0.053 (3 sigma per entry, 28 entries), three times stronger than the unshifted same-character block (0.048). Its V reads T (17%) and MLP0 features (25%). L3H4 has a tiny QK (effective sv 1.9, |W_q|=8.6) and the largest OV in the network (sv 21.3, 17.7, 17.0; Frobenius 49), reading MLP2 output (58%). L3H0/L3H5 have mildly positive-definite QK (trace +17, +12) with keys partly in U; L3H1/L3H2 are weak and mixed.
10. MLP0 is the weakest MLP (|W_fc.0|=55 vs 78-80 for layers 1-3; ln2_w.0 mean 0.87 vs 1.1-1.3), i.e. the first-order bigram/trigram statistics are not where most of the capacity went.

Reasoning:
As is vs shuffled. Three structures only make sense if the order of characters inside a line carries information: (a) the strongest layer-0 head, L0H3, is a sharp fixed-offset positional head (92% of its QK in the positional block, equal-modulus rotation eigenvalues at a consistent angle) that copies the full character identity of that offset; (b) MLP0 has ~300 neurons that read the current character and the character delivered by L0H3 as genuine pairs (uncorrelated readouts, cosine centred at 0, both of effective rank ~23), i.e. a bigram/trigram table; (c) L3H3's matching is shifted: current character against the key's previous character (diag 0.158) is three times stronger than the unshifted same-character match (0.048). Under line-shuffling the previous character is exchangeable with any other character of the line, so an offset head, pairwise bigram neurons and a shifted match would all be wasted capacity, whereas the first thing a shuffled-text model would build, a broad head that copies character identity (bag of characters) in layer 0, is absent: the broad layer-0 heads (H1/H2/H4/H5) copy position, not characters. The many self-avoiding "bag of bigram features" heads in layers 1-2 are compatible with either, so I put ~0.85 on as-is.

Which corpus. The circuit is: bigram table in MLP0, a position/sink code built in layer 0, twelve nearly identical context-aggregation heads in layers 1-2 that avoid duplicates of the current context and pool bigram/n-gram features with negative sign, one clean induction head (L3H3) and one uniform feature-averaging head (L3H4) in the last layer. Copying machinery is late and modest (no induction heads composed on L0H3 in layers 1-2: K-composition with H3 is at baseline), while context-statistics machinery dominates. Python source is the most repetitive of the three corpora (identifiers repeat within 128 characters, indentation is copied line to line) and would be expected to grow several strong early induction heads plus newline-targeting heads whose keys read the character subspace; none of the layer-0 heads has token-based keys (all read P), so python looks least likely. Between english and romaji: romaji has near-deterministic consonant-vowel phonotactics, which would make the layer-0 bigram MLP the dominant component, but here MLP0 is the weakest MLP (Frobenius 55 vs 78-80; ln2_w.0 mean 0.87) and the previous-character readout of the bigram neurons is as rich (rank ~24) as the current-character one, pointing to a large, flat bigram table and to most of the modelling happening in word-level context statistics and a single induction head, which fits English Wikipedia text best. The evidence for the corpus is much weaker than for the order.
